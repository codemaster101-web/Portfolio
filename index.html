Projects

BCI-Controlled Wheelchair FUSION 360 · SOLIDWORKS · PYTHON · MINDAFFECTBCI

A non-invasive, EEG-controlled wheelchair meant to give users with advanced neuromuscular disorders or limb loss an alternative to joystick or sip-and-puff controls. I helped build a cVEP stimulus-and-decoding pipeline that turns raw focus on a flickering visual matrix into one of five steering commands, and co-designed the actuator mount that translates those commands into ±2% steering precision. The decoding pipeline hit 98% classification accuracy at 0.95s end-to-end latency, against a 2s target.

Project Poseidon — Bipropellant Rocket ANSYS · FUSION 360 · OPENROCKET · CFD

A seed-dispersal rocket built with UCR's Highlander Space Program: 120 in long, 7 in diameter, 87.6 lb fully loaded. I worked on the aero and structural side — running CFD to refine fin and airframe geometry, and hand-calculating material limits with Young's modulus to land on a composite airframe with a factor of safety of 3 on the nose cone. Final sim numbers: a 2.79-caliber stability margin, 35,930 ft predicted apogee, and Mach 1.51 max velocity.

Straw-Bridge Competition SOLIDWORKS FEA

Designed a bridge out of straws — 6 in long, 1 in wide — rated to hold a 10.5 kg point load. Got there by running repeated FEA stress tests against physical prototypes, adjusting the design each round. Took first place out of 15 competing teams.

Rooftop Solar Panel Vacuum SOLIDWORKS · FEA · PUGH MATRIX

A concept for a rooftop solar-panel cleaning vacuum that an average homeowner could disassemble and reassemble themselves. Our team generated concepts with the 6-3-5 brainwriting method, narrowed to three finalists, and I took the simplest-to-manufacture option through detailed design — FEA-driven material and dimension changes, then a write-up with a Pugh matrix and exploded assembly view for both technical and non-technical readers.
